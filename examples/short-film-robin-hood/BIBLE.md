# Story bible: The Golden Arrow

## Style line (in every character, still and scene prompt)

`storybook ink and watercolour illustration, bold ink outlines, warm painted colours, medieval folk-tale style`

Original designs inspired by the public-domain folk tale; they intentionally do not imitate any film or comic adaptation.

## Characters (descriptors never change)

| Name | Descriptor to repeat in scenes |
|---|---|
| `robin` | green hooded tunic, feathered cap, brown leather belt, longbow |
| `little-john` | bald, thick red beard, rust-brown tunic with rolled sleeves, oak quarterstaff |
| `sheriff` | thin waxed moustache, plum velvet doublet with gold trim, tall feathered hat |

## Voice bible (paste verbatim before every line)

| Speaker | Voice phrase |
|---|---|
| Narrator (off-screen) | warm deep unhurried male voice |
| `robin` | cheeky young confident male voice |
| `little-john` | booming good-natured deep male voice |
| `sheriff` | pompous nasal male voice |

## Ambience (reuse the same phrases across cuts)

- Forest: rushing stream, birdsong, creaking log.
- Tournament: crowd murmur, trumpet fanfare, flapping pennants.

## Film grammar

Establishing → medium two-shot → close-up. Continuous action uses `continuesFrom` and opens "Continuing directly from the previous shot…". The location change (bridge → tournament) is a hard cut using a location still as `keyframes.first`.

# Screenplay: The Golden Arrow

*Based on the traditional English folk tale of Robin Hood (public domain). About 50 seconds, 6 scenes.*

---

**EXT. SHERWOOD FOREST – LOG BRIDGE – DAWN**

Mist over a rushing stream. A mossy log bridge, no one on it.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
In Sherwood, every road had a toll, and every toll had a thief.

ROBIN, a lean young archer in a green hooded tunic, steps onto the bridge, longbow on his back.

**CONTINUOUS – THE BRIDGE**

LITTLE JOHN, a giant forester with a red beard and an oak quarterstaff, blocks the far end.

LITTLE JOHN
Nobody crosses this bridge without paying the toll!

ROBIN
(grinning)
Then you'll have to take it from me.

Robin sets down his bow.

**CONTINUOUS – THE BRIDGE**

They fight with staves on the narrow log. Robin ducks, cartwheels, and taps Little John into the stream with a huge splash. John surfaces, roaring with laughter.

LITTLE JOHN
Well fought, lad!

**EXT. VILLAGE GREEN – ARCHERY TOURNAMENT – AFTERNOON**

*Hard cut.* Striped tents, a cheering crowd. THE SHERIFF, pompous, in plum velvet, presides from a pavilion.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
The Sheriff's prize was a golden arrow. He never expected anyone to win it fairly.

SHERIFF
No one shoots better than my archers.

**CONTINUOUS – THE FIRING LINE**

Robin steps up and draws. The crowd falls silent. His arrow splits the arrow already in the bullseye. The crowd erupts.

**CONTINUOUS – THE PAVILION**

SHERIFF
Seize that archer!

Guards stumble over each other. Robin snatches the golden arrow.

ROBIN
Thank you kindly!

He and Little John vault the fence and run into the trees.

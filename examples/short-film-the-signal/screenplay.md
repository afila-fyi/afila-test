# THE SIGNAL

*A short film, about two minutes.*

## SEQUENCE 1: THE CALL

INT. LIGHTHOUSE LAMP ROOM - NIGHT (STORM)

A storm rages outside. MAYA (30, grey braid, blue wool sweater) writes in a logbook by a marine radio. The radio crackles.

TOMAS (V.O., radio)
Mayday... anyone... this is the Marigold... we are losing the engine.

MAYA
Marigold, this is Saint Brigid light. Hold on. I'm lighting the lamp.

She throws the big brass lamp lever. The beam ignites and begins to turn.

## SEQUENCE 2: THE STORM

EXT. FISHING BOAT - NIGHT (STORM)

A huge wave breaks over the bow. TOMAS (50, white beard, green oilskin, sailor cap) fights the wheel. The engine dies.

TOMAS
Mayday, mayday! This is the Marigold, we've lost the engine!

A distant beam cuts through the rain. His face lights up with relief.

INT. LIGHTHOUSE LAMP ROOM - NIGHT

The lamp blazes.

MAYA
I see you, Marigold. Steer for the light. Keep it just off your starboard bow.

She walks to the window and watches the beam sweep the sea.

MAYA (whispering)
There you are.

## SEQUENCE 3: DAWN

EXT. STONE DOCK - DAWN

Calm sea. The battered boat is moored. Tomas ties the rope and looks up.

EXT. LIGHTHOUSE BALCONY - DAWN

Maya, now in a yellow raincoat, raises a mug toward the dock.

EXT. STONE DOCK - DAWN

Maya walks down the steps. Tomas removes his cap.

TOMAS
That light of yours. I thought I'd never see land again.

MAYA
That's what the light is for. Come up, the kettle's on.

TOMAS
Lead the way, keeper.

They climb the steps together toward the sunlit lighthouse. The lamp is dark. FADE OUT.

TITLE: THE LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER

FADE IN:

EXT. COASTAL CLIFF - DAWN

A white lighthouse stands on a green cliff above a calm sea. The sun
rises. MAYA (30s, grey braid, yellow raincoat) steps onto the balcony
with a steaming mug. PIP, a small seagull, lands on the railing.

                    MAYA
          Another day, another storm that never came.

Pip tilts its head and squawks.

EXT. OPEN SEA - LATER

Clouds roll in fast. The sea turns dark and choppy. Far below, a tiny
red rowboat is tossed by the waves.

                    MAYA (O.S.)
          Pip... that's a boat.

INT. LIGHTHOUSE LAMP ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Maya cranks the great lamp. A beam of light sweeps across the water.
Pip flaps out of the window toward the boat.

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

The beam finds the rowboat. A child waves from it. Pip circles
overhead guiding the way. The waves calm as the lamp light holds.

                    MAYA
          (smiling)
          Told you. Never trust a quiet morning.

FADE OUT.
